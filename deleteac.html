How to Request Account Deletion

1

Open Your Email App

Compose a new email to ramanghorpade2069@gmail.com

2

Use the Correct Subject Line

Set the subject to: Account Deletion Request — Vermillion View

3

Include Your Account Email

In the email body, include the email address you used to sign in to Vermillion View with Google. This helps us locate your account quickly.

4

Send & Wait for Confirmation

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation email to confirm your account has been deleted.

What Data is Deleted

  • Your account profile — name, email address, profile photo Deleted
  • Photographs you uploaded that have not been purchased by other users Deleted
  • Your in-app search history and activity data Deleted
  • Advertising identifiers linked to your account Deleted

What Data is Retained and Why

  • Completed transaction records — Retained for 5 years to comply with Indian financial regulations (GST and Income Tax Act) Retained 5 years
  • Photos purchased by other users — Buyers retain access; your name is removed from the listing within 30 days Anonymised in 30 days
  • Razorpay payment records — Governed by Razorpay's own data retention policy Razorpay policy

Delete Specific Data Only (Optional)

If you wish to delete only certain data — such as specific uploaded photos, your activity history, or your profile details — without closing your account, you may do so by sending the same email and specifying which data you would like removed. We will action partial deletion requests within 7 business days.
