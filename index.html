Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 27, 2026  ·  Last Updated: February 27, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Vermillion View ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our photo marketplace application. By using Vermillion View, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
1

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our services:

Account Information
Name, email address, and profile photo provided via Google Sign-In
Uploaded Content
Photos you choose to upload for sale, including titles, descriptions, and tags
Transaction Data
Purchase history, payment confirmation IDs, and transaction amounts processed via Razorpay
Usage Data
App interactions, search queries, and browsing activity within the app
Device Information
Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
Advertising Data
Ad interaction data collected by Google AdMob for serving advertisements
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
  • To enable you to upload, publish, and sell photographs on our marketplace
  • To process payments and maintain accurate purchase and sales records
  • To deliver purchased photographs to buyers and provide download access
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob (maximum 3 per day)
  • To improve the performance, security, and features of the application
  • To communicate important updates, transaction confirmations, and support responses
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
3

Third-Party Services

Vermillion View integrates with trusted third-party services to deliver core functionality. Each service operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and file storage. Your data is stored on Google's secure cloud infrastructure. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Razorpay — Payment processing for photo purchases. We do not store your card details; all payment data is handled directly by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant systems. razorpay.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — In-app advertising. AdMob may use device identifiers and usage data to serve personalised advertisements. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — Account creation and login. Only your name, email, and profile photo are shared with us. policies.google.com/privacy
4

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers, which maintain industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. We implement Firebase App Check to protect our backend services from unauthorised access.

While we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Google account.

Payment Security: Vermillion View does not store, process, or have access to your credit card, debit card, or banking details. All financial transactions are processed entirely by Razorpay under their security framework.

Photo Uploads and Intellectual Property

When you upload a photograph to Vermillion View, you represent and warrant that you own the copyright to that image or have the necessary rights to sell it. You retain full ownership of your photos.

By uploading, you grant Vermillion View a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your photographs within the app for the purpose of facilitating sales. We do not sell, license, or transfer your photos to any third party beyond what is necessary to operate the marketplace.

Purchased photographs are delivered to buyers for personal use only. Resale or redistribution of purchased images is prohibited under our Terms of Service.

6

Advertising

Vermillion View displays interstitial advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Advertisements are shown a maximum of 3 times per day per user. After this limit is reached, no further ads are displayed until the following day.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant advertisements. To opt out of personalised advertising, you can adjust your device's advertising settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Opting out does not remove ads entirely; it limits personalisation based on your profile
